1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A Nunavut
   employee gets the territorial income tax on the *Provincial Income Tax
   (NU)* line, the deduction *Nunavut Payroll Tax*, the hidden lines of the
   Nunavut remuneration and of the WSCC assessable payroll, and the
   employer line *WSCC Assessment (Nunavut)* when the company has a rate
   and the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the territorial income tax, not the payroll tax.
3. Remit the payroll tax withheld (lines *Nunavut Payroll Tax*,
   contribution register *Government of Nunavut - Payroll Tax*) to the
   Department of Finance by the 20th day of the month after the reporting
   period assigned to the company, and file the annual return, even nil,
   by February 28. A credit note takes the payroll tax of the pay back.
4. Report the payroll to the WSCC (Annual Payroll Report by February 28):
   the hidden lines *WSCC Assessable Payroll (Nunavut)* of the payslips
   give the assessable payroll.

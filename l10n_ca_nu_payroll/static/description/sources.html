Guide to Payroll Tax Withholding and Reporting (December 2011) Government of Nunavut, Department of Finance Employers withhold 2% of the gross remuneration of each employee. Not subject: members of religious orders with a vow of poverty, and less than 5,000 a year earned in Nunavut by an employee who normally works outside it; withhold once the 5,000 can reasonably be expected to be exceeded. Remittance by the 20th day of the month after the reporting period; annual return, even nil, by February 28. l10n_ca.nu.payroll_tax.rate Verified
Nunavut Payroll Tax Application for Registration (February 2022) Government of Nunavut, Department of Finance A withholding or pay-as-you-earn payroll tax of 2% of all gross remuneration paid to the employees who work more than half their time for an employer in Nunavut, collected by the employer from each employee. Registration within 21 days after the first remuneration paid. l10n_ca.nu.payroll_tax.rate Verified

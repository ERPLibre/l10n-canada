1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A New
   Brunswick employee gets the New Brunswick income tax on the
   *Provincial Income Tax (NB)* line, the hidden lines of the New
   Brunswick remuneration and of the WorkSafeNB assessable earnings, and
   the employer line *WorkSafeNB Assessment* when the company has a rate
   and the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the New Brunswick income tax.
3. Report the payroll to WorkSafeNB (Form 100 by February 28, or monthly
   by the 15th of the following month): the hidden lines *WorkSafeNB
   Assessable Earnings* of the payslips give the assessable earnings.

1. On the employee holding the majority of the voting shares, if any,
   check *Majority Voting Shareholder* in the *Settings* tab.
2. Confirm the payslips of the year (state *Done*). Their worked days
   lines must hold the hours: they are filled when the employee, the
   contract or the dates of the payslip are set in the form.
3. Open Invoicing (Accounting) > Canada > Corporate Tax > Quebec Remunerated
   Hours and create the record of the fiscal year. *Hours from Payroll* shows the
   counted payroll hours, already included in *Hours of the Company*.
4. Add manual lines only for hours not paid through payroll: unpaid hours
   worked by a majority shareholder, or employees paid outside Odoo.
5. Create the record of the preceding year too, with the hours of the
   associated corporations: the preceding-year test reads it.

Invoicing (Accounting) > Canada > Sales Tax Returns:

1. Create a return: the type (GST/HST or Quebec FPZ-500), the period and the
   method default from the company profile. The period defaults to the last
   complete reporting period.
2. *Compute*: the lines follow the form. The magnifier of a line opens the
   journal items it sums. Under the quick method, the overridden lines are
   flagged and a note details eligible supplies, remittance and credit.
3. Enter the amounts in NETFILE or in *Mon dossier* of Revenu Québec.
4. *Post Closing Entry*: the tax accounts of the period are cleared against
   the account of the authority. The payment to the authority is then
   recorded against that account.
5. *Mark as Filed*, with the filing date and the confirmation number.

*Reset to Draft* deletes the lines and the closing entry.

On a posted customer invoice, the *Bad Debt* button writes off the unpaid
balance, or records the recovery of an amount written off. The adjustment
appears on lines 104/107 and 204/207 of the return of the period of the
entry.

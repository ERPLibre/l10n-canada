1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A Yukon
   employee gets the Yukon income tax on the *Provincial Income Tax (YT)*
   line, the hidden lines of the Yukon remuneration and of the WSCB
   assessable earnings, and the employer line *WSCB Assessment* when the
   company has a rate and the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Yukon income tax.
3. Report the payroll to the WSCB (annual payroll report by the last day
   of February, or monthly by the 15th of the following month under the
   actual payroll method): the hidden lines *WSCB Assessable Earnings* of
   the payslips give the assessable earnings of each worker.

All the entries are under Invoicing (Accounting) > Canada > Corporate Tax.

- *Compute Shareholder Loans*: choose a fiscal year and the date of the
  situation; the list shows, per shareholder, the deadline, the unpaid
  amount and the deemed interest. Enter the interest paid by the
  shareholder within 30 days after the calendar year end. The daily
  scheduled action refreshes the current and preceding fiscal years.
- *Quebec Remunerated Hours*: one record per fiscal year with the hours of
  each employee, and the hours of the associated corporations.
- *Corporate Tax Estimates*: create the estimate of the fiscal year, click
  *Load from Accounting*, complete the adjustments (capital cost allowance)
  and the preceding-year figures, then *Compute Instalments*.
- *Dividends*: create, *Declare* (resolution entry), then *Pay*.
- *Generate T5 and RL-3 Slips*: choose the calendar year, download the CSV
  file, and copy the boxes into the CRA T5 web forms and the Revenu Quebec
  RL-3 online service. The XLSX workbook is in the *Print* menu of the slip
  list.

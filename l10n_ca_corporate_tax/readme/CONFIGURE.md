1. Invoicing (Accounting) > Configuration > Settings > Canadian Tax Profile: check that
   the company is a CCPC and choose the instalment frequency.
2. Same page, *Owner-Manager Corporate Tax*: dividends declared account
   (equity), dividends payable account (reconcilable liability), dividend
   declaration journal, GRIP balance from schedule 53, reminder delay and
   responsible user of the shareholder loan reminders, and the Quebec
   income tax instalment frequency when it differs from the federal one
   (the quarterly conditions of Revenu Quebec are not those of the CRA).
3. Chart of accounts: tick *Shareholder Loan* on the account(s) that hold
   the amounts owed by shareholders, and set the *Corporate Tax Treatment*
   of the meals and entertainment, non-deductible and investment income
   accounts.
4. Contacts: tick *Shareholder* or *Connected to a Shareholder* in the
   accounting tab.
5. Invoicing (Accounting) > Configuration > Canadian Time Parameters: review the rates
   (codes `l10n_ca.corp_tax.*`, `l10n_ca.qc_sbd.*`, `l10n_ca.dividend.*`)
   and add a version when a budget changes one.

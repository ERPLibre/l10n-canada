1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A
   Northwest Territories employee gets the territorial income tax on the
   *Provincial Income Tax (NT)* line, the deduction *Northwest Territories
   Payroll Tax*, the hidden lines of the Northwest Territories remuneration
   and of the WSCC assessable payroll, and the employer line *WSCC
   Assessment (Northwest Territories)* when the company has a rate and the
   contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the territorial income tax, not the payroll tax.
3. Remit the payroll tax withheld (lines *Northwest Territories Payroll
   Tax*, contribution register *Government of the Northwest Territories -
   Payroll Tax*) to the Department of Finance by the 20th day of the month
   after the reporting period assigned to the company, and file the annual
   return by February 28. A credit note takes the payroll tax of the pay
   back.
4. Report the payroll to the WSCC (Annual Payroll Report by February 28):
   the hidden lines *WSCC Assessable Payroll (Northwest Territories)* of
   the payslips give the assessable payroll.

1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A Quebec
   employee gets the QPP, QPIP, Quebec income tax lines and the employer
   contributions (QPIP, health services fund, labour standards, CNESST).
2. Payroll > Canada > RL-1 Year-End (Quebec): create the summary of the
   year and click *Compute Remittances* to get, per period of the Revenu
   Québec frequency, the amounts of the remittance slip (QPP, QPIP, income
   tax, health services fund, CNESST) with their due date. Record the
   amount paid on each remittance.
3. After the last pay of the year, click *Compute RL-1 Slips*. Enter the
   eligible training expenditures (WSDRF) and the labour standards
   contribution already paid if any, check the RL-1 summary lines and
   export the CSV files to fill in the online services of Revenu Québec
   before the last day of February. Line 82 is the balance to pay with the
   summary (labour standards, WSDRF and the adjustment of the health
   services fund to the actual rate).
4. The T4 slips of the federal module now carry boxes 55 and 56 for the
   Quebec employees; the Quebec tax stays out of box 22.

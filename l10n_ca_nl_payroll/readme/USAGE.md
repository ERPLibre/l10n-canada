1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A
   Newfoundland and Labrador employee gets the provincial income tax on
   the *Provincial Income Tax (NL)* line, the hidden lines of the
   Newfoundland and Labrador remuneration and of the WorkplaceNL
   assessable earnings, the employer line *Health and Post-Secondary
   Education Tax (2.000%)* once the remuneration of the year exceeds the
   exemption, and the employer line *WorkplaceNL Assessment* when the
   company has a rate and the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Newfoundland and Labrador income tax.
3. Health and Post-Secondary Education Tax: file the monthly return and
   pay the tax within 20 days after the end of each month once the
   remuneration of the year exceeds the exemption; the employer lines of
   the month give the tax and the hidden lines *Newfoundland and Labrador
   Remuneration* the gross remuneration of line 1 of the return.
4. Report the payroll to WorkplaceNL with the Employer Payroll Statement
   due February 28 (actual payroll of the previous year and estimate of
   the current one): the hidden lines *WorkplaceNL Assessable Earnings*
   give the assessable earnings, the excess above the maximum already
   left out.

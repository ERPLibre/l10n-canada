1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A British
   Columbia employee gets the British Columbia income tax on the
   *Provincial Income Tax (BC)* line, the hidden lines of the British
   Columbia remuneration and of the WorkSafeBC assessable earnings, the
   employer line *British Columbia Employer Health Tax* once the
   remuneration of the year exceeds the exemption of the company, and the
   employer line *WorkSafeBC Premium* when the company has a rate and the
   contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the British Columbia income tax.
3. Employer Health Tax: pay the instalments (June 15, September 15 and
   December 15) when the tax of the previous year exceeded 2,925, and file
   the return by March 31 of the following year. The hidden lines
   *British Columbia Remuneration* of the payslips give the remuneration
   of the year; the lines of the tax are an accrual that the return sets
   on the actual remuneration.
4. Report the payroll to WorkSafeBC (annually in March, or quarterly by
   April 20, July 20, October 20 and January 20): the hidden lines
   *WorkSafeBC Assessable Earnings* give the assessable earnings.

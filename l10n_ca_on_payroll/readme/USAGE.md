1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. An Ontario
   employee gets the Ontario income tax on the *Provincial Income Tax (ON)*
   line, the hidden lines of the Ontario remuneration and of the WSIB
   insurable earnings, and the employer lines *Ontario Employer Health Tax*
   (once the exemption is used up) and *WSIB Premium* (when the company has
   a WSIB rate and the contract is covered).
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Ontario income tax.
3. Remit the Employer Health Tax to the Ontario Ministry of Finance: the
   lines *Ontario Employer Health Tax* of the payslips of a month give the
   monthly instalment of an employer whose annual total Ontario
   remuneration exceeds 1,200,000; the others pay the tax of the year with
   the annual return. The annual return recomputes the tax on the actual
   total Ontario remuneration of the year.
4. Report and pay the WSIB premiums at the frequency of the company: the
   hidden lines *WSIB Insurable Earnings* of the payslips of the period give
   the insurable earnings to report, the lines *WSIB Premium* the premium.

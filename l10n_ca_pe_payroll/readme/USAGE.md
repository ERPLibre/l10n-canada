1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A Prince
   Edward Island employee gets the Prince Edward Island income tax on the
   *Provincial Income Tax (PE)* line, the hidden lines of the Prince
   Edward Island remuneration and of the WCB PEI assessable earnings, and
   the employer line *WCB PEI Assessment* when the company has a rate and
   the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Prince Edward Island income tax.
3. Report the payroll to WCB PEI: the registration renewal by February 28
   (actual payroll of the previous year and estimate of the current
   year), or the monthly payroll with each payment under the monthly
   assessment payment option; payroll revisions by November 1. The hidden
   lines *WCB PEI Assessable Earnings* of the payslips give the assessable
   earnings.

Insurance Rates and Premiums Workers' Compensation Board of Nova Scotia Premium = rate per $100 x assessable payroll, the gross earnings of the covered workers each up to the yearly maximum. Each employer is notified on September 1 of its rate for the next calendar year. Verified
How Assessment Rates are Set Workers' Compensation Board of Nova Scotia Rate of an employer = rate of its industry rate group adjusted by experience rating, plus the safety association levy and a surcharge where they apply. Average rate 2.65 per $100 of assessable payroll, 2.25 from January 1, 2027. Verified
Payment and Reporting Workers' Compensation Board of Nova Scotia Monthly or quarterly report of the assessable payroll, by pay date, with the payment, due the 15th of the month that follows the reporting period, even for a nil payroll. Verified
Do You Need WCB Coverage Workers' Compensation Board of Nova Scotia Coverage mandatory in a mandatory industry with 3 or more workers at the same time; officers and directors active in the business count as workers; proprietors and partners are not workers and may buy optional coverage. Verified

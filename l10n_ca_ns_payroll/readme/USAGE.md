1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A Nova
   Scotia employee gets the Nova Scotia income tax on the *Provincial
   Income Tax (NS)* line, the hidden lines of the Nova Scotia remuneration
   and of the WCB Nova Scotia assessable earnings, and the employer line
   *WCB Nova Scotia Premium* when the company has a rate and the contract
   is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Nova Scotia income tax.
3. Report the assessable payroll to WCB Nova Scotia by pay date, with the
   payment, by the 15th of the month after the monthly or quarterly
   period (the General Regulations align it on the CRA remittances): the
   hidden lines *WCB Nova Scotia Assessable Earnings* of the payslips give
   the assessable earnings.

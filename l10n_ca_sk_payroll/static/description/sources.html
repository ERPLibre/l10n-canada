Employer Premium Payments (POL 25/2025) Saskatchewan Workers' Compensation Board Assessable earnings: gross earnings before deductions, up to the maximum of the year. Industry premium rate: a dollar amount per 100 of assessable payroll. Premiums of 5 or more are payable on the statement of account, due 30 days after it and/or on April 1 and September 1 depending on the annual assessment. l10n_ca.sk.wcb.max_insurable To verify
The thresholds of the April 1 and September 1 due dates are in the appendix of PRO 25/2025, not read.

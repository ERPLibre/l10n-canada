1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A
   Saskatchewan employee gets the Saskatchewan income tax on the
   *Provincial Income Tax (SK)* line, the hidden lines of the Saskatchewan
   remuneration and of the WCB assessable earnings, and the employer line
   *Saskatchewan WCB Premium* when the company has a rate and the
   contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Saskatchewan income tax.
3. File the Employer's Payroll Statement with the WCB by February 28
   (actual assessable wages of the previous year, estimate of the current
   year): the hidden lines *Saskatchewan WCB Assessable Earnings* of the
   payslips give the assessable earnings of each worker, already capped at
   the maximum of the year. Pay the statement of account on the due dates
   it gives.

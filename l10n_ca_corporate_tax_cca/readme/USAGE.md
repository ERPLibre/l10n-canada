1. Generate the federal CCA schedule of the fiscal year (Invoicing (Accounting) >
   Assets > CCA Schedules), and adjust the CCA claimed if less than the
   maximum is wanted.
2. Open the corporate tax estimate of the same fiscal year and click *Load
   from Accounting*. The automatic lines of type *Capital cost allowance*
   come from the schedule; manual lines are kept.
3. After any change to the schedule, click *Load from Accounting* again: a
   warning on the estimate says when the loaded lines are out of date.

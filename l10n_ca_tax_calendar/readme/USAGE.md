Invoicing (Accounting) > Canada > Generate Deadlines creates the deadlines right away;
the scheduled action *Canada: Generate Tax Deadlines and Reminders* does it
every day.

Invoicing (Accounting) > Canada > Tax Deadlines lists what is due, with the calendar
and kanban views. Open a deadline to read the legal rule and its sources,
note the confirmation number of the filing or payment in *Reference*, and
click *Mark as Done*. Marking the *Tax Deadline* activity done in the
activity menu has the same effect.

A change of the tax profile is taken into account at the next generation:
the future deadlines of rules that no longer apply are cancelled and the
new ones are created.

The calendar is a reminder tool. Some dates depend on facts the module
does not know (taxable income of the associated group, average monthly
withholding of two years before, notices received from the authorities):
check the notices of the CRA and Revenu Quebec when the profile changes.

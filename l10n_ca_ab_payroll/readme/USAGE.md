1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. An
   Alberta employee gets the Alberta income tax on the *Provincial Income
   Tax (AB)* line, the hidden lines of the Alberta remuneration and of the
   WCB-Alberta assessable earnings, and the employer line *WCB-Alberta
   Premium* when the company has a rate and the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Alberta income tax.
3. File the WCB-Alberta annual return (January 1 to February 28): the
   hidden lines *WCB-Alberta Assessable Earnings* of the payslips give the
   assessable earnings of each worker, already capped at the maximum.

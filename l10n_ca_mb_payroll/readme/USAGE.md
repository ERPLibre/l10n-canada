1. Compute and confirm the payslips as with the federal module. A
   Manitoba employee gets the Manitoba income tax on the *Provincial
   Income Tax (MB)* line, the hidden lines of the Manitoba remuneration
   and of the WCB Manitoba assessable earnings, and the employer lines
   *Health and Post Secondary Education Tax Levy* once the remuneration
   of the year exceeds the exemption, and *WCB Manitoba Premium* when the
   company has a rate and the contract is covered.
2. The CRA remittances (PD7A) and the T4 slips of the federal module
   include the Manitoba income tax.
3. File the levy return and pay the levy to Manitoba Finance (TAXcess)
   by the 15th of the following month once the remuneration of the year
   exceeds the exemption; the employer lines of the month give the
   accrual. The annual report, due March 31 of the following year,
   settles the levy on the actual remuneration of the year.
4. Report the payroll to WCB Manitoba before the last day of February:
   the hidden lines *WCB Manitoba Assessable Earnings* of the payslips
   give the assessable earnings.
